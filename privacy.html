Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Introduction

Generated Media, Inc. ("we", "our", "us") operates Inkdraft: Tattoo Stencil AI (the "App"), an iPhone app that turns your photos into tattoo stencils and draws tattoo sketches from a text description. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and who receives it.

In short: the App has no account and no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Stencils from your photos are made on your device, and your photos are never uploaded. When you ask for sketches, the text you write, the style you choose, and, for similar sketches, the sketch you picked are sent to the Icons8 image generator to draw them. Everything you save stays on your device.

Your Photos and Stencils

  • You choose a photo with the system photo picker or take one with the camera. The picker gives the App only the photo you select; the App does not get access to the rest of your photo library. The camera is used only while you take that photo.
  • The stencil is calculated on your device. Your photo and the stencil are not sent to us, to the image generator, or to anyone else, and the App does not save your photo: it is held in memory while you work on the stencil.
  • A stencil leaves the App only when you choose to save it to your photo library, save it as a PDF to the Files app, print it, or share it.

Sketch Generation

When you tap to create sketches, the App sends the following to the Icons8 image generator, operated by Icons8, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • Your prompt - the text you wrote describing the tattoo, with fixed style instructions the App adds to it.
  • The style you chose and a random number used to vary the result.
  • The reference sketch - only when you ask for sketches similar to one you have, the image of that generated sketch.

The App does not send your photos, your saved sketches list, your name, or any device or advertising identifier with these requests, and it keeps no cookies for them. Like any internet service, the generator receives your device's IP address when the App connects to it. The generator returns the images, which the App shows on your device. Please do not write personal information, such as names or contact details of real people, in your prompts.

Prompts and reference sketches are used to generate your sketches and to check them against the content rules: the generator does not return sexual or explicit images.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following in its private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Saved sketches - the image of each sketch you save to "My sketches", with its prompt, style, and the date you saved it.
  • App settings - whether you have finished the introduction, the printer you used last, how many free stencils you have exported, and the App version in which it last asked you for a rating with the number of saves since then.

The App does not sync this data with iCloud or any server. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup) if you have them enabled; that is a function of iOS, not of the App, and we have no access to those backups. Deleting a sketch in the App removes it; deleting the App removes everything it stored.

Permissions

  • Camera - asked the first time you take a photo for a stencil.
  • Photos (add only) - asked the first time you save a sketch or stencil to your photo library. It lets the App add images to your library; it does not let the App read your library.
  • Notifications - asked when you start generating sketches, so the App can tell you that your sketches are ready if you leave it while they are being drawn. This notification is created on your device and shows your prompt; the App does not use push notifications from a server.

You can change any of these in iOS Settings → Inkdraft at any time. Declining a permission only turns off the feature that needs it.

Printing, Saving, and Sharing

Stencils are printed through the standard iOS print dialog (AirPrint). Save PDF opens the standard iOS dialog where you choose a folder in the Files app; if you pick an iCloud Drive folder, the file is stored there under your Apple Account. For thermal stencil printers, the App prepares an image file and hands it to your device's share sheet, where you choose the printer app. Sketches and stencils you share go wherever you send them through the share sheet. Once you send them to another app or service, that recipient's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App receives from Apple the list of plans with their prices and which plan, if any, is active on your Apple Account. We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details.

Rating Prompt

After you save a sketch or a stencil, the App may show Apple's standard prompt asking you to rate it. Any rating or review you leave is submitted to Apple under your App Store account; we do not receive it through the App.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require an account, login, email address, or phone number - the App has no sign-up of any kind.
  • We do not upload your photos or the stencils you make from them.
  • The App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and it does not ask for permission to track you.
  • We do not collect device or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not collect location. The App requests no location permission.
  • We do not read your photo library, contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • We do not have access to your payment information.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising.

Sharing of Information

The prompts, styles, and reference sketches described under "Sketch Generation" are sent to the Icons8 image generator, operated by Icons8, to draw your sketches. Apple receives what is needed to process your purchases. We do not share the content you create in the App with anyone else.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal process.

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA and UK Users)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process your prompts, chosen styles, and reference sketches to perform our contract with you: you ask for sketches, and we need this information to draw them. We rely on our legitimate interests to check requests against the content rules and to prevent abuse of the image generator. The data controller for the App is Generated Media, Inc.

Your Rights (EEA and UK Users)

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Your photos, stencils, and saved sketches are stored only on your device, so you control them directly: delete a sketch in the App, or delete the App to erase everything. Prompts sent to the image generator are not linked to your name or to any account, because the App has none. You may contact us using the details below with any question about your data, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

International Data Transfers

The image generator and its infrastructure may process your prompts and reference sketches in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Data Retention

Content you save is kept on your device for as long as you keep it there and is deleted when you delete it or uninstall the App. Prompts and reference sketches sent to the image generator are used to produce your sketches. The generator's servers may keep requests and their technical logs for as long as needed to provide the service, keep it secure, and prevent abuse; they are not linked to your name or to any account.

Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

The only information that leaves your device in connection with the App is the content of your sketch requests (prompts, styles, and reference sketches), which may count as user content, and the IP address your device uses to connect. It is used only to provide sketch generation. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Data Security

Your App data is protected by the security of your own device, including the device passcode and iOS app sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading the App's storage. Sketch requests are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes affecting how your data is handled will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any of the rights described above, please contact Generated Media, Inc. at generated.photos/contact-us.
